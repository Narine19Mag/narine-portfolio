How they're built

[ 01 ]

Welcome flows

The first email a subscriber ever sees, carrying the code that converts them. Brand voice set immediately, one clear offer, and the hero product introduced before the reader has to scroll.

[ 02 ]

Promotional campaigns

Sitewide offers where the discount has to read instantly at thumbnail size in a crowded inbox — large type, product-forward imagery, and the button placed where the thumb already is.

[ 03 ]

Product education

Ingredient and fabric breakdowns that explain why a product works — annotated callouts, numbered routine steps and benefit labelling that turn a spec sheet into something worth reading.

[ 04 ]

UGC & community

Giveaways built around numbered entry steps so the mechanics are understood at a glance, with layouts that hold user-generated photography without looking like a different brand.

[ 05 ]

Modular merchandising

Product grids that scale from three items to thirty without redesign — consistent card spacing, price and title hierarchy, and per-product CTAs that survive reflowing to a single column.

[ 06 ]

Built for real inboxes

Every layout collapses cleanly to one column on mobile, stays readable when images are blocked, and keeps type at a size that works on a phone held at arm's length.
