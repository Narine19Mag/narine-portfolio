Email & UI Design

Email & Lifecycle Design

Welcome flows, promotional campaigns, product education and retention design for e-commerce brands across beauty, swimwear, activewear and haircare — built to stay on-brand in the inbox and legible on a phone.

DisciplineEmail & lifecycle
Built inKlaviyo
FormatsFlows & campaigns
[ 01 ]

Welcome & onboarding flows

The first email a subscriber ever sees, carrying the discount code that converts them. Brand voice set immediately, one clear offer, and a single obvious action — with the hero product introduced before the reader has to scroll.

[ 02 ]

Promotional campaigns

Sitewide offers and seasonal sets where the discount has to read instantly at thumbnail size in a crowded inbox. Large type, product-forward imagery, and a button placed where the thumb already is.

[ 03 ]

Product education

Ingredient and fabric breakdowns that explain why a product works — annotated callouts, numbered routine steps, and benefit labelling that turns a spec sheet into something worth reading.

[ 04 ]

Community & UGC campaigns

Giveaways and creator campaigns built around numbered entry steps, so the mechanics are understood at a glance. Layouts designed to hold user-generated photography without looking like a different brand.

[ 05 ]

Merchandising & best sellers

Modular product grids that scale from three products to thirty without redesign — consistent card spacing, price and title hierarchy, and per-product CTAs that survive being reflowed to a single column.

[ 06 ]

Built for real inboxes

Every layout is designed to collapse cleanly to one column on mobile, stay readable when images are blocked, and keep type at a size that works on a phone held at arm's length.
