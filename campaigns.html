06 — Selected Work

Social & Campaign Design

Social and campaign design across e-commerce and creator brands — key art, post sets and placement systems built so one idea survives being resized into every format it has to ship in.

[ 01 ]

Colour as the hook

A single saturated field does the work of stopping the scroll, with the product and the people kept in full colour against it. The palette is loud on purpose and stays consistent across every placement.

[ 02 ]

Creator-first composition

Real creators lead the frame rather than product shots. Layered, overlapping cards suggest a feed of people rather than a catalogue, and the stack reads as depth even at small sizes.

[ 03 ]

A modular card system

Each card holds an avatar, handle, creator category and one action — a repeatable unit that can be restacked, recoloured or extended to any number of creators without redrawing the layout.
